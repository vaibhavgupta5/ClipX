Market friction

Problem statement

High freelance editing costs and tedious manual tools keep solo creators from scaling.

02 / 07
₹500–₹1,000
Prohibitive editing costs in India

A freelance reel editor charges ₹500 to ₹1,000 per video in India. A solo creator publishing 20–30 reels a month to grow spends ₹10,000 to ₹30,000 every month, out of reach for students, beginners, and micro-influencers.

2–3 hrs
The manual editing tax

Self-editing a 45-second reel takes 2 to 3 hours: trimming silences, aligning captions word by word, centering face crops, scouring meme archives.

6-inch screen
Clunky mobile editing UX

Desktop NLEs are overpowered for rapid vertical output. Mobile editors force creators to pinch, split, and scrub millisecond audio boundaries with a thumb on a six-inch screen.

Consistency gap
Creator burnout & stagnation

Social algorithms reward daily consistency. When each video costs hours of scrubbing or hundreds of rupees, creators burn out and post intermittently.

Strict safety gate

The LLM never writes shell commands or raw FFmpeg filters. It edits a strongly typed timeline, so an unvalidated filter injection isn’t possible.

Instant undo / redo

Every agent action creates an immutable timeline version in TimescaleDB. Reverting an edit is a zero-cost pointer move.
